Drop your real resume here as `resume.pdf`.

While this folder has no resume.pdf, the Resume app typesets the structured
`resume` data from src/data/portfolio.ts as a paper document instead, and the
download button hands over a plain-text version.


`poster.jpg` is the picture in the frame on the right of the back wall of the
3D room. Swap it for whatever you like.

It is fitted into the space on the wall without being stretched, so any aspect
ratio works — portrait suits the wall best, and around 3:4 fills the frame
exactly. Keep it near 900px wide: the wall is small on screen, and anything
larger only costs load time. While this folder has no poster.jpg, the frame
comes off and the room shows a plain tinted sheet in its place.
